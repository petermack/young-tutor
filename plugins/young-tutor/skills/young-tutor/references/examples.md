# Examples

Use these examples as a model. Change them to suit the child, the school programme and the interests of the child.

## Example ladder 1: phonics

Child: age 4, Reception, England. Programme: Little Wandle Letters and Sounds Revised. Interest: farm animals.

| Level | Goal | Example question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hear the first sound in a spoken word | "Sun, sock, seal. What sound do they start with?" (/s/) |
| 2 | Say the sound for s, a, t | Show the letter t: "What sound does it make?" |
| 3 | Say the sound for s, a, t, p, i, n | Show the letter p: "What sound does it make?" |
| 4 | Blend spoken sounds into a word | Parent says /p/ /i/ /g/: "What animal is it?" |
| 5 | Read CVC words with s, a, t, p, i, n | Read: pin, sat, tap |
| 6 | Read CVC words with m, d, g, o, c, k | Read: dog, cat, map |
| 7 | Read a short caption | Read: "a pig in a pit" |

## Example ladder 2: number bonds

Child: age 5, Year 1, England. Programme: White Rose Maths. Interest: football.

| Level | Goal | Example question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Count up to 10 objects, and touch each one | "How many footballs? ⚽⚽⚽⚽⚽⚽" |
| 2 | Subitise up to 5 (dice patterns) | Show 4 dots quickly: "How many?" |
| 3 | Number bonds to 5 with objects | "5 players. 3 are on the pitch. How many are on the bench?" |
| 4 | Number bonds to 5 without objects | "3 and what make 5?" |
| 5 | Number bonds to 10 with a ten frame | "7 counters on the ten frame. How many spaces?" |
| 6 | Number bonds to 10 without objects | "6 and what make 10?" |
| 7 | Use a bond to 10 to add | "8 + 2 = ?, so 8 + 3 = ?" |

## Example ladder 3: a topic that is not a school topic

Child: age 6, any country. Topic: how plants grow. Interest: the garden.

| Level | Goal | Example question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name a plant and a thing that is not a plant | "Is a daisy a plant? Is a stone a plant?" |
| 2 | Name the parts of a plant | Look at a real plant: "Point to the leaf." |
| 3 | Say what a plant needs | "What does a plant drink?" |
| 4 | Put the life cycle in order | "Seed, flower, shoot. Which comes first?" |
| 5 | Plan a test | "How can we find out if a bean needs light?" |
| 6 | Say what happened and why | "The bean in the cupboard is yellow. Why?" |

## Example session (under 7)

Child: Bee, age 4. Topic: phonics, level 3. New idea: the sound /p/.

**Tutor:**

> Hello! Today Bee practises /s/, /a/ and /t/, and learns one new sound: /p/. It takes about 5 to 10 minutes.
>
> **Parent:** Put 3 things on the table: a sock, a teddy and an apple.
>
> **Say:** "Hello Bee! Let's play I Spy. I spy a thing that starts with /s/."
>
> (Say "sss", not "suh".)
>
> **Type:** the thing that Bee picks.

**Parent:** she picked the sock

**Tutor:**

> Well done, Bee! You listened very carefully.
>
> **Parent:** Now the new sound. Hold your hand in front of your mouth. Whisper a quick /p/. Feel the small puff of air. Do not say "puh".
>
> **Say:** "This is /p/. Can you feel the puff? Now you try."
>
> **Type:** what Bee says.

**Parent:** she said buh

**Tutor (a hint with a real object):**

> Nearly! That was /b/. /p/ is a whisper.
>
> **Parent:** Get a small piece of paper or a feather. Hold it in front of Bee's mouth.
>
> **Say:** "Whisper /p/. Can you make the paper move?"
>
> **Type:** what happens.

**Parent:** the paper moved! she said p

**Tutor:**

> Brilliant, Bee! You made the paper move. You kept trying!

Later, in practice, Bee gives 2 correct first tries in a row. Level 4 (blending) is a second new idea, so the tutor does not teach it today:

> **Parent:** Bee got 2 right, so Bee is ready for level 4. We start level 4 next time. Now one more fun question with /p/.

At the end, the tutor saves the progress file:

- `what_worked`: "A piece of paper to feel the puff of air for /p/"
- `what_did_not_work`: "Only the spoken sound for /p/: Bee said /b/"
- `next_step`: "Level 4: oral blending with p-i-g, s-a-t, t-i-p."
